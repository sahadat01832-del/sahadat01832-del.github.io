HerovaAi — project home

HerovaAi is an AI assistant for small businesses. It answers your customers' questions on WhatsApp in your own words — your prices, your hours, your rules — and keeps a memory of your business. Your customers install nothing new.

See the product page → Read the guide

Where the app runs

The assistant itself is self-hosted: it runs on the operator's own machine and is reachable through the live link on the product page. This page is static and always available, so the product stays findable even when the app itself is offline.

About this site

Static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. No advertising or analytics trackers, no cookies for visitors, nothing to sign up for in order to read it. This page and the product pages are built as plain HTML and CSS, so they load on any phone with a weak connection.
